## What Salesforce charges

From [salesforce.com/au/sales/revenue-intelligence/pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/au/sales/revenue-intelligence/pricing/):

- Revenue Intelligence: AU$308 (US$220) per user per month, billed annually: insight dashboards, deal insights and an analytics platform.
- Revenue Intelligence with Tableau: AU$350 (US$250) per user per month, billed annually.
- "This edition requires an annual contract."
- Revenue Intelligence is included in the Max edition of Sales Cloud, or bought separately. It sits on top of a Sales Cloud licence.

From [salesforce.com/sales/pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/pricing/): Sales Cloud Core AU$273, Advanced AU$553, Max AU$770 per user per month, billed annually.

## What a forecast run actually is

The weekly forecast in most B2B teams is the same five jobs: snapshot the pipeline, see what moved, inspect the commit deals, take each rep's call, and compare the total with quota and any published guidance. At quarter end the calls are compared with what was won. Each of those is a command here.
